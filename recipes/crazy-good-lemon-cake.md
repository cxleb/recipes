# Crazy Good Lemon Cake

## Ingredients
- 1 Whole Lemon
- 3 eggs
- 160g sugar
- 50g whole milk
- 65g neutral oil
- 1 tbsp vanilla bean paste 
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 250g Ap flour
- 2 1/2 tsp baking powder
- A couple of small sticks of butter (like 3x3x40mm)

For glaze
- 1 Cup powdered sugar
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 2 Tbsp whole milk (may need to add more depending on thickness preference)
- Zest of 1 lemon

## Method
1. Preheat oven to 160c
2. Boil lemon for 5 minutes, once boiled cool for a few minutes
3. Chop lemon into small cubes and remove seeds
4. Combine lemon cubes, eggs, sugar, milk, oil, vanilla, and salt and blend until a slurry
5. Fold in flour and backing powder
6. Line and grease a loaf tin with baking paper and butter
7. Transfer to tin and place slivers of butter on top
8. Bake for 50 min
9. Cool on wire rack
10. Combine ingredients for glaze and cover cooled but warm loaf.
