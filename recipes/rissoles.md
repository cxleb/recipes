# Rissoles

It's not a very strict recipe, you can add more or less vegetables to stretch your meat. Zuchinni and parsley is optional, can add if you want more vegetables or leave out if you dont have it.

The most important part is getting the right "wettness", you need it to be fairly moist and pastey but not like wet, its a large amount of egg so add breadcrumbs until its paste like.

Usually served with large amounts of gravox gravy :) and mash or chips.

## Ingredients
1kg lean beef mince
1 onion, finely diced
2-3 garlic cloves, crushed
2 carrots, grated
1 Zuchinni, grated (Optional)
Parsley, finely chopped (Optional)
1 cup panko breadcumbs (I mostly use a random amount so just add by feel)
1 tbsp Worchestershire Sauce
2 large eggs, beaten
1 tsp Chicken or Beef stock powder
1 tsp Pepper

## Method
1, Preheat oven to 200c
2, Lightly caramelise the onion in a pan on low heat with some oil, about 5min.
3, Once done, add garlic and carrot, fry it off until fragant and the oil is orange. put aside to cool down.
4, Combine in a bowl everything else but only add about half the breadcrumbs, mix it up a bit and add the carrot/garlic/onion.
5, Mix everything fairly well, try to homogenise the fat but not too much other theyll become springy, add more breadcrumbs until right texture is achieved.
6, Shape into golf-ball sized balls, really doesnt matter about how perfect or well shaped they are but along as theyre meatball like.
7, Place on wire rack and bake for about 20min or until its browned on the top(idk looks good looking?)
